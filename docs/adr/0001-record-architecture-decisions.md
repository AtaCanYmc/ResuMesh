# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-07-09

## Status

Accepted

## Context

We need to record the architectural decisions made on this project.
Without documentation of our architectural decisions, it can be difficult for future contributors and team members to understand why certain technical choices were made. This leads to repeated discussions, hesitation in refactoring, or unintentional deviations from the intended architecture.

## Decision

We will use Architecture Decision Records (ADR) to capture important architectural decisions.
Each ADR will follow a standard format including Title, Date, Status, Context, Decision, and Consequences. We will store these files in the `docs/adr` directory.

## Consequences

- Improved onboarding for new developers.
- Clear historical context on why certain technologies or patterns were adopted.
- Requires discipline from the team to write an ADR whenever a significant architectural change is made.
